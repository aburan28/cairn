James A. Garfield, twentieth President of the United States, was shot on
2 July 1881 at the Baltimore and Potomac Railroad Station in Washington,
D.C. He died of his wounds on 19 September 1881 at Elberon, New Jersey.
Charles J. Guiteau was convicted of the killing and executed in 1882.
