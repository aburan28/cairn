# Code of conduct

This project takes contributions from strangers, runs their code, and pays for
results. It works because people can rely on each other's good faith in public.
That is the whole of the reasoning below.

## What is expected

- **Argue about the work, not the person.** Technical disagreement here is
  routine and welcome — this repository ships a second implementation whose
  entire job is to disagree with the first. Direct it at the code, the record,
  or the claim.
- **Be honest about what you know.** Overstating what is defended is the one
  thing this project cannot afford; [docs/threat-model.md](docs/threat-model.md)
  marks every attack **handled / partial / not handled / unsolvable** and
  keeping it honest is a stated project rule. The same applies in conversation:
  say what you measured, and say what you did not.
- **Assume the other person has a reason.** When a design looks wrong, ask what
  the constraint was before saying it is wrong. Most of the comments in this
  codebase exist because the obvious alternative had a failure mode.
- **Accept a correction gracefully, and give one kindly.** Being wrong in public
  is the normal cost of working in the open.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption, in issues, pull requests,
  commit messages, review comments, or any other project space.
- Insults, demeaning comments, or attacks based on who somebody is — including
  age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or
  expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberate bad faith: knowingly false claims about what code does, fabricated
  results or benchmarks, or submissions designed to waste reviewers' time.
- Using the project's channels to abuse people elsewhere.

## Scope

Everywhere this project happens: the repository, its issues and pull requests,
its discussions, and any space where you are representing the project. It also
covers conduct outside those spaces when it is directed at someone because of
their participation here.

Conduct *toward the network* — what an objective may ask for, what a submission
may claim, and what a node may do with code it is given — is a separate matter
governed by the protocol rules in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and enforced by the
verifier, not by this document. An objective's statement is untrusted text; that
is a security property, not a courtesy.

## Reporting

Report privately to the repository owner ([@aburan28](https://github.com/aburan28)),
using the contact details on their GitHub profile. Include what happened, where,
and links if the conduct is in a public space. You will get an acknowledgement
and you will not be asked to confront the person yourself.

If the report concerns the maintainer, or you would rather not go through them,
GitHub's own [abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) is the
escalation and it is outside this project's control.

Reports are handled in confidence. A reporter's identity is not shared with the
person reported without the reporter's agreement.

**This project has no dedicated conduct address yet.** That is a gap, not a
policy, and a maintainer should replace this section with one when there is a
mailbox to name — the same way [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) names the advisory
form rather than pretending to have an email address it does not have.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, commits, code, and other
contributions that violate this document, and will say why when they do.
Repeated or severe violations lead to a temporary or permanent ban from the
project's spaces.

Enforcement decisions are made by the maintainers, are proportionate, and are
explained to the person concerned. Nothing here is automatic, and nothing here
is a substitute for a maintainer using judgement.
